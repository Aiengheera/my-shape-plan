Simple portion guide

Protein

Include eggs, turkey, fish, chicken, beans or yoghurt at meals.

Starches

Keep rice, pasta, yam, plantain and swallow in satisfying, moderate portions.

Vegetables & fruit

Add vegetables at lunch and dinner and include fruit most days.

Oil, sauces and drinks

Measure oil and groundnuts; account for sugary drinks and rich sauces.

Weekly training plan

Repeat × 8 weeks
Warm up for 5 minutes. Rest 60–120 seconds between sets. Start with easy variations and stop for sharp pain, dizziness, chest pain or unusual breathlessness.

How to progress

Weeks 1–2: learn the movements. Weeks 3–4: add a few repetitions or light resistance. Weeks 5–6: progress gradually while keeping good form. Weeks 7–8: continue consistently and review measurements. Don't add resistance and repetitions all at once.
